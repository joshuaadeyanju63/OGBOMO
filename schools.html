Schools sorting competition

A proposed pilot: classes compete to sort the most clean recyclables, and learn why it matters.

Idea stage. We have not run a school competition yet. Tell us if your school would like to try one in a pilot.

How the competition would work

  1. Each class sorts plastic bottles, pure water sachets, cans, glass and paper at school for a term.
  2. Staff weigh each class's material at a drop-off point and confirm the kilograms.
  3. The class with the most clean, sorted material wins a prize chosen with the school, such as books, sports kit or a tree-planting day.
  4. Sales of the material fund the prize and the next round. Pupils learn why sorting matters through our education guides.

The idea is based on school programmes run by TerraCycle and Nigerian recyclers such as RecyclePoints. Prizes and prices are not decided.
