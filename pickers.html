For people who collect recyclables

If you collect bottles, sachets, cans, glass or paper for a living, a pilot would pay you cash by weight at a drop-off point, with the weight written down in front of you. This is the model RecyclePoints uses for waste pickers. Prices would come from a real buyer and we would show them to you before you start.

  • Collectors report being paid ₦250 to ₦620 per kg for clean PET by recyclers in 2025 (Nairametrics). Transport and rejected dirty bottles cut into that, so we want to understand your costs.
  • We cannot promise a price yet.
